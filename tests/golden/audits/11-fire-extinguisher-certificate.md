Company context to scan under: Cascade Specialty Chemicals, LLC · industry: chemical distribution · 4410 NW Front Ave, Portland, Oregon 97210 · one site, "Portland".

## ANSWER KEY

Run three times. Passes when every "must" holds in all three.

Identity (must):
- kind: certificate
- issuer: Rose City Fire Protection, Inc. (a service company, not an agency)
- agency includes a fire authority for Portland — accept "Portland Fire & Rescue", "Portland Fire Marshal", "Oregon State Fire Marshal" or "OSFM" — or OSHA (1910.157 governs portable extinguishers). Whichever the scan chooses, the label string must be the same across the three runs.
- subject includes something like "fire protection" or "fire extinguishers"
- site: Portland
- doc_date: August 20, 2025, kind "issued"; expiry August 20, 2026

Status (must): the runner computes it from the dates and the run date: expiring within 90 days before August 20, 2026; expired after. At a run date of September 29, 2026 or later: expired. Never "compliant".

Gaps (must): none on the certificate itself. It is a service record and is complete.

Deadlines (must contain, with dates):
1. Next annual maintenance August 20, 2026 (flagged as passed at any run date after it)
2. Six-year maintenance for W-3 and L-1 by November 2026

Facts (may contain, quote verified true): 14 extinguishers at the Portland facility as of August 20, 2025; the service company's name and licence. Acceptable.

Must not: a gap that monthly inspections are missing; the certificate says they are the customer's responsibility and this document does not record them. "compliant" anywhere.

## AUDIT USE

Once this document is on file, an audit under the fire authority (or Oregon OSHA, whichever label the readings gave it) must say:
- Annual extinguisher maintenance: on file, stale. Certified August 20, 2025, next due August 20, 2026, passed; nothing on file since. What to do: have the units serviced and add the new certificate.
- Six-year maintenance for W-3 and L-1: due November 2026; nothing on file.
- Monthly visual inspections: not a document question unless the tags are photographed; nothing on file, and the certificate says they are recorded on the unit tags.
- This document makes a third agency group on the Audits page. If the readings label it Oregon OSHA instead, it sits under Oregon OSHA and the page has two groups; either is correct, and the label must not change between readings.
