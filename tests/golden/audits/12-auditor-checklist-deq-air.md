## ANSWER KEY

Run three times. Passes when every "must" holds in all three.

Identity (must):
- kind: other, template or checklist, whichever the vocabulary has; never permit, policy or record
- issuer: Oregon DEQ (it is the agency's blank form); the agency label may be Oregon DEQ or none; the scan may say this is the agency's form rather than the company's document
- subject: air quality, or none
- site: Portland, or none
- doc_date: none, or "undated"; a scan that invents a date fails the run

Status (must): not_judged, or the vocabulary's equivalent for a document that is not evidence about the company. Never gaps_found, never "compliant".

Gaps (must): none. Every line in the form is a question, not a finding. A gap on this document is a false finding and fails the run.

Deadlines, conditions, facts: none required. A scan that proposes "renewal 120 days before expiration" as a fact about Cascade from this document fails the run; the form states no fact about Cascade.
