## ANSWER KEY

Run three times. Passes when every "must" holds in all three.

Identity (must):
- kind: other, template or checklist, whichever the vocabulary has; never policy, record or program
- issuer: Cascade Specialty Chemicals (the company's own form)
- agency: none, or a mix of Oregon DEQ and Oregon OSHA; either passes. The label strings, if any, must be the same across the three runs and match the company's existing labels
- site: Portland
- doc_date: none, or "undated"; a scan that invents a month fails the run

Status (must): not_judged, or the vocabulary's equivalent for a blank form. Never gaps_found, never "compliant".

Gaps (must): none. The lines are checks, not findings.

Deadlines, conditions, facts: none required. A scan that proposes "eyewash stations are flushed weekly" as a fact about Cascade fails the run; the form asks whether it was done and says nothing about whether it was.
