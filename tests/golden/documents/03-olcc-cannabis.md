## DOCUMENT TEXT

OREGON LIQUOR AND CANNABIS COMMISSION
9079 SE McLoughlin Blvd, Portland, Oregon 97222

RECREATIONAL MARIJUANA PRODUCER LICENSE

License number: 020-1004763A8B2
Licensee: Evergreen Botanicals, LLC
Trade name: Evergreen Botanicals
Licensed premises: 2210 Bailey Hill Road, Eugene, Oregon 97405
License type: Producer — Tier II, Indoor
Canopy limit: 10,000 square feet mature plant canopy
Original issue date: March 3, 2022
Current term: March 3, 2026 to March 2, 2027
Expiration date: March 2, 2027

This license is issued under ORS 475C and the rules of the Commission in OAR chapter 845, division 25, and authorizes the licensee to produce marijuana at the licensed premises named above, subject to those rules and to the conditions below.

Conditions of this license:

1. The license is valid only for the premises named above and is not transferable to any other location or person.

2. The license must be renewed annually. A renewal application and the renewal fee must be received by the Commission no later than 20 days before the expiration date. A licensee that has submitted a complete renewal application before that date may continue to operate until the Commission acts on it.

3. All marijuana items at the premises must be tracked in the Cannabis Tracking System, and every employee who handles marijuana items must hold a current marijuana worker permit issued by the Commission.

4. Video surveillance meeting OAR 845-025-1450 must be maintained at the premises and recordings kept for at least 90 days.

5. Any change in ownership, in the premises, or in the persons with a financial interest in the licensee must be reported to the Commission before it takes effect.

This license must be displayed at the licensed premises where it can be seen by the public.

Issued by the Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission
Date of issue for the current term: March 3, 2026
Authorized signature: ____________________  Licensing Director

## ANSWER KEY

Run three times. Passes when every "must" holds in all three.

Identity (must):
- kind: certificate or permit (either; the row shows "Licence" if the scan's title says so). The title should contain "producer" and "license" or "licence".
- issuer / agency: OLCC (accept "Oregon Liquor and Cannabis Commission", "OLCC"); the label string identical across the three runs. "Oregon Liquor Control Commission" is the old name and is a near miss to note, not a failure.
- site: Bailey Hill (accept the address)
- doc_date: issued March 3, 2026 (current term); expiry March 2, 2027
- jurisdiction: Oregon

Status (must): current (before December 2, 2026) or expiring (within 90 days) depending on the run date; never "compliant".

Gaps (must): none. Any gap is a false finding.

Deadlines (must contain): renewal application due 20 days before expiry, that is February 10, 2027 (condition 2); expiry March 2, 2027. The scan must state February 10, 2027 or "20 days before March 2, 2027"; either passes.

Conditions (must contain): premises-only, not transferable (1); Cannabis Tracking System and worker permits for every employee handling product (3); video surveillance with 90-day retention (4); report ownership or premises changes before they take effect (5).

Facts (must contain, quote verified true): canopy limit "10,000 square feet" (page 1); licence type Tier II indoor. Acceptable inferred fact: the company has employees who handle marijuana items (only if marked inferred).

Expected-missing (correct if present, labelled as based on similar companies): worker permits for each employee, a surveillance system record, Metrc/CTS records. Not required.
