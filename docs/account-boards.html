My Account and the front door, drawn in the shared frame

Eight boards for a desktop window 1280 wide. The sample company is Willamette Vinegar Works, Maria is signed in, and today is 21 October 2026. Five tabs on My Account in the shared tab row — Company, People, Sign-in, Billing, Cancel — and the drawer for anything edited (a site, an invitation, a password), as everywhere else in the app. Then the front door: a three-field signup, the onboarding walk in three screens, and what a company sees after the trial with no card.

7 · The onboarding walk, three screens720 × 1240

Screen 1 is the website card when a site was given — the read proposes the company name, what they do and the addresses it found; nothing is written until "Yes, that's us"; "Let me fix it" turns each line into a box. With no website, screen 1 asks the one thing still missing: the main site's address (the name came from signup). Screen 2 is the Dashboard's question card, three times. Screen 3 is done. Every screen can be left; the Dashboard's own card continues the questions another day.
