Audits gets the shared chat

Draft 3, 3 November 2026 — three tabs; the owner's example lines and paperclip wording are in. Five boards. The chat and its conversations are the shared screen Documents and Calendar use — the same box, answer, action row and conversation list. The third tab is today's two tabs folded into one: every audit, by agency and by template, with the Audit again buttons; the box that started audits moves into the Ask tab and keeps working exactly as it does, in code, with no model.

The sample company is Willamette Vinegar Works. Every answer here is sample text in the product's voice, not a reading of a real audit. The two example questions on board 1 are the owner's own words from 2 November ("what is our status on all documents related to OSHA", "I see gaps in three reports, how to solve it"). Green boxes are decisions; amber boxes are questions only the owner can answer.

I see gaps in three reports. How do I fix them?

Three fixes, in the order that matters: the coordinator, the forklift evaluation, the missing roster.

  1. Name a new emergency coordinator in the contingency plan. The plan names D. Ruiz, who the audit says has left.1 Oregon’s hazardous waste rule asks for a named coordinator who can be reached at all times.3 Change section 4, add a phone number, and date the revision.
  2. Re-evaluate R. Patel on the forklift. The last evaluation is dated May 2023; the rule asks for one at least every three years.24 A short observed evaluation, signed and dated, closes it.
  3. Add the hazardous waste training roster. Nothing on file. If training happened, add the sign-in sheet; if it did not, it needs to, and the roster comes from it.

When the three are done, run the Oregon OSHA audit again and the lines turn.

What the Audits chat is given — for the brief, the owner's yes

In this order, with a budget and what did not fit said out loud, the Documents chat's rule: who the company is; every audit run — one line each: agency, date, documents read, counts by word; the findings of the latest run per agency — one line per finding with its four words, the document it points at, and the fix; the index of every document, one line each, the same index the Documents chat uses; the full reports of the documents the question names and of the documents the named findings point at. Never the files, except one the person attaches. Offers: none for rev 1.
