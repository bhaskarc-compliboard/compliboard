Yes, that's us Skip for now
  1. Pressing change turns that one line into a box holding the current words. They write what is true. No list, no suggestions: if the scan did not get it, we do not guess twice.
  2. The same for the sentence and the address: each becomes its own box when asked, and only that one.
  3. "Done" closes the box and the line reads back as written. The card can be confirmed with any line open or closed.

What changes behind the card. The model's answer gains the industry line, written from our short list of industries (chemical, food and beverage, cannabis, pharmaceutical, cosmetics and personal care) and roles (manufacturing, distribution, both); when the site fits none, it says so and the line is blank with "write it". We keep the sentence (about, new) and the industry line as the person left it (what_you_do, the field the Company tab already has), plus the model's structured picks, which are cleared the moment the person writes their own words. The NAICS code leaves the front door: it stays in the proposal as evidence and nothing on the walk writes it. The Company tab shows the same two lines with the same change. Which requirement library a company is served stays a separate, unseen column (chemical for everyone today); the industry the model found is what will choose it when there is more than one.
