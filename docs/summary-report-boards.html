The summary report — partitions and a little colour

Draft 2, 4 November 2026 — Still to confirm in blue, the owner's call. The report a person gets from "Summarise this conversation", the same component in six places: the Workspace, HR, the Calendar, Documents, Audits and the conversation drawer. Drawn here in the drawer over the Audits Conversations tab, on a Clackamas conversation about the air permit. Two boards: as it opens, and with everything opened.

The rule: What applies is the report — it opens first with the first authority open and the rest folded; everything else is one counted row until asked for. Each section is its own card with a coloured left edge and a tinted header, so the eye finds the partitions before it reads a word: grey for your situation, green for what applies, blue for still to confirm — the colour of "tell us", not of a fault, so amber keeps meaning one thing everywhere — and pale grey for the rest.
