The Documents list and the document drawer

Draft 4, 5 November 2026 — friendlier header lines ("things we noticed — our reading, not the page's words"); breathing room inside every card (the owner: this is the demo report); a fourth board with Notes and Dates opened. Three boards on the owner's own Clackamas files: the list by date added — the new default — with a batch reading at the top; the same list by status; and a document's reading as the drawer, with the summary report's partitions.

The rules drawn here: the banner is gone; the default group is Date added, newest first, so a batch lands together; the batch's progress is on its group header, not in a box; a row leads with a short name (the reading's title to its first clause — never the address or the company name the reader appended), with the file name small beneath and the agency in its own column; the status word, its date and the notes count in fixed columns (59). One short-name rule, used by the list, the drawer, the summary's sources and the binder.

What it says

1. Rohan Patel's three-year evaluation is overdue

Last evaluation 2 February 2023; the three-year evaluation was due by 2 February 2026, about eight months before the roster was signed as current. Nothing on the roster shows a later evaluation.

Fix. Have a qualified person evaluate him on the sit-down LP counterbalance truck now and update the roster and his card. Do not let him operate unsupervised until that is done.

29 CFR 1910.178(l)(4)(iii), adopted in Oregon through OAR 437-002-0227 · page 1, operator table, row 1

Needs work · 2 gaps read from the page · 2 notes · Record · OSHA · last entry 1 October 2026 · read 3 November 2026

What it says

The company's own list of its forklift operators: four operators, the truck types each runs, the date each was first certified, the date of their last evaluation and who did it. It is a record, so it is judged on whether it is complete and up to date. Two of the four operators are past their three-year evaluation, even though the page says it is current as of 1 October 2026.

▸Gaps read from the page2
▾Notes2 things we noticed

1. The roster says it is current but it is not

The header says every operator is evaluated at least once every three years; the table shows two past that interval. The record contradicts itself, and an inspector reading it would see it straight away.

Fix. Once the re-evaluations are done, reissue the roster. Consider a 'next evaluation due' column so overdue dates are easy to see.

29 CFR 1910.178(l)(4)(iii) and (l)(6) · page 1, header paragraph

2. Training dates and the trainer are not shown separately from the evaluation

The certification must show the operator's name, the training date, the evaluation date, and who did each. This roster gives an initial certification date and an evaluator, but no separate training date or trainer — those may be on the cards in the warehouse office.

Fix. Check the cards. If they lack training dates and the trainer's name, add those to the cards or to this roster.

29 CFR 1910.178(l)(6) · page 1, operator table and footer
