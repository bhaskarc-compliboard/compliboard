What’s wrong with this plan?

Most of it holds. Three things the rule asks for are missing or will go stale.

This is the written emergency action plan for the Portland plant, revised February 2025.1 Measured against OSHA's rule for these plans:2

  1. No way to account for everyone after an evacuation. Section 4 says where people assemble but not who counts them. The rule asks for procedures to account for all employees after evacuation. Add a headcount step and name who does it.12
  2. The contacts are named people, not job titles. Section 7 names two people to contact about the plan. That meets the rule today, but names go out of date when people leave; the rule allows the job title instead. If either person has left, the plan needs changing now.12
  3. One alarm for every emergency. The plan calls the alarm "the bell" for fire, spill and shelter alike. The rule asks for a distinctive signal for each purpose. Say which signal means evacuate and which means shelter.12

Want me to draft section 4's headcount step, or turn these three into a checklist?

Read whole for this answer, and filed in Documents with its own report.

Where a person lands when another section sends them here — decided 2 November

Documents reads the link it was opened with and picks the tab; the fix lives here, in one place. The sidebar and the bare address open Ask a question. A link that asks for a file to be added (?add=) or says where to come back to (?return=) opens the Documents tab; Documents reads both words today (app/documents/page.tsx:185, :198).

Found while checking, and to be confirmed with one click before it is called a defect: links that name one document (/documents?document=<id>) are written by Company information beside each fact and by the Calendar chat's source cards, and nothing in the Documents page reads that word. So those links land on Documents and open nothing. Documents learns it here: the Documents tab opens with that document's report in the drawer, which board 2's source cards need anyway.

Four links carry no word at all, so Documents cannot know what they meant, and each needs one word in its own file (or becomes an upload in place, now that every section can upload): Audits' "Go to Documents", the Workspace after a new version is uploaded, the Dashboard's "Add a document" when nothing is upcoming, and the document-batch email's "See them all". Those are changes in four other sections; the map names each, and each section's free checks run again.
