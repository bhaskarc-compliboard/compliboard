1 March 2027, and it is on the calendar for Portland only.

7 · Dates to confirm1280 × 1300

Step 14b: a second section sits under the queue — "Needs a day or how often (N)", every live date with no day at all, grouped the same way, each row opening the drawer. It has no Confirm and no picker: the question there is WHEN. A date can be in both, and that is deliberate — the queue asks "is this right?", the section asks "when?", and both are open about the two rows below. The tab's count stays the queue's (14 here); the section carries its own. Grouped by the document each date was read from, so a first scan's flood is a few clicks: "Confirm all" on the group heading, or Confirm and Not right on a row. Every row is also on the Calendar tab already; this tab only shortens the work. An audit's dates get their own group. Dates added from the chat or typed in are confirmed already, so they never appear here.

8 · Not right, and Remove720 × 460

One sheet, two titles. "Not right" on an unconfirmed date and "Remove" on any date both offer a one-line reason, optional: Remove works with nothing typed. What stops the same document proposing the date again is that a person removed it, recorded with who and when; the reason, if given, shows in the history. Cancel has the focus, so Enter never removes.

Remove this date?
