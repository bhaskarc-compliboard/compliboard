The landing page
1 · The Dashboard, a company with history1280 × 1240

One column, the owner's order. The welcome is the page title: "Welcome back, Maria" for anyone who has opened the Dashboard before, the person's first name from their profile, or plain "Welcome back" when there is none. The numbers are hairline cells, not cards, so the page has one surface. One question at a time, right under the numbers, so the one thing we want from the person is above the fold. Upcoming and Recent audits follow as their own sections, each with a serif heading and its way out on the right. Upcoming shows up to six dates in the window and says how many more there are; Recent audits shows the last three. A date row opens the Calendar's drawer; an audit row opens that report on the Audits page.

The three questions
2 · The question card, four states720 × 760

One card, one question at a time, 1 of 3 to 3 of 3. The question's words and the grey line under it come from the switch itself, so the Company information page and the Dashboard ask in the same words. A yes-or-no switch gets two buttons; a number gets a box and Save; a choice gets one button per option. "I don't know yet" moves to the next question and leaves the switch unanswered. After the third answer the card goes quiet until another day. Nothing says what the answer switched on.

Day one
3 · The first visit, nothing in the app yet1280 × 960

"Welcome, Maria" the first time this person opens the Dashboard, "Welcome back" every time after; one timestamp on the person's profile, set on the first view, decides which. Four dashes, no zeros and no coaching line. Upcoming and Recent audits each say what will appear there, with the way to make it happen. The questions are the one block that works on day one, so this is where they earn their place: a company that has uploaded nothing can still answer three questions.

The sidebar
4 · The sidebar720 × 600

The green box goes. The owner's order: Dashboard, Compliance Workspace, Audit Workspace, HR Workspace, Calendar, Documents; a thin line; Company information and My Account under it. The active page is marked exactly as the workspaces mark their active tab — the same two greens, the same 2px line: the word in the dark green (--brand-strong), the underline in the brand green (--brand) — so one rule says "you are here" everywhere in the app. "Company Documents" becomes "Documents"; "Audits" becomes "Audit Workspace". The counts are plain small grey numbers, no pill, shown only when something waits for a person. Built 20 October (commit 901b835), measured identical on every page's column.
